Free fixed asset register in Excel with Companies Act and Income-tax depreciation

Keep one asset list and get two depreciation schedules from it: Companies Act depreciation for your books and audit, and block-wise Income-tax depreciation for the tax return.

What's included

  • Asset-wise register with purchase and put-to-use dates, location, cost and method
  • Schedule II useful lives filled in automatically, with an override for each asset
  • Straight-line (SLM) and written-down value (WDV) depreciation, pro-rata by days and stopping at residual value
  • Disposals with profit or loss on sale
  • Class-wise summary ready for the property, plant and equipment note, with a check that it agrees to the register
  • Income-tax block of assets with half-rate depreciation for assets used under 180 days

How to use it

  1. Set the financial year dates on the Settings tab.
  2. Add each asset on the Asset Register tab and pick its class, method and Income-tax block.
  3. For older assets, enter accumulated depreciation up to the start of the year.
  4. Review the Summary tab for your accounts and the IT Depreciation tab for the tax return.

Blue cells on a yellow background are inputs; everything else is a live formula, so totals and results update as you type. Sample figures are filled in so you can see how it works.

Questions

How is the WDV rate worked out?

Under the Companies Act, the WDV rate is 1 − (residual value ÷ cost) raised to the power 1 ÷ useful life. With a 5% residual value and a 10-year life, this gives about 25.89%.

Does it handle the 180-day rule for Income-tax?

Yes. Assets put to use for less than 180 days in the year get half the block rate. Additional depreciation and other special provisions are not modelled; adjust those manually if they apply.

Can I change the useful life of an asset?

Yes. Each asset has a 'Life override' column. Use it where a different life is justified by technical evaluation, and disclose it in your accounts.
