Company & ROC compliance

Annual MCA filings, director KYC and event-based changes for private limited companies, OPCs and LLPs, on time and with DSCs issued in-house.

FY 2025-26 extension. MCA General Circular 05/2026 (24 October 2026) extended AOC-4 to 31 December 2026 and MGT-7 / MGT-7A to 31 January 2027, without additional fees. AGM, ADT-1 and DIR-12 timelines were not extended.

ROC annual filing

Every company has to file its financial statements and annual return with the Registrar of Companies each year, even if there was no business. We prepare the forms, attach the documents and file them with the directors' DSCs.

  • AOC-4 (and XBRL / CFS where applicable)
  • MGT-7 / MGT-7A annual return
  • LLP Form 8 and Form 11
  • Board report and AGM documents coordination

Usually needed: Signed financial statements, auditor's report, board report, shareholder list and directors' DSCs.

DIR-3 KYC & DIN

Annual KYC keeps a director's DIN active. We also handle new DIN applications and surrender of DIN.

  • DIR-3 KYC (form and web)
  • New DIN application
  • DIN surrender

Usually needed: Director's PAN, Aadhaar, mobile, email and DSC.

Post-incorporation filings

Filings new companies often miss in the first months.

  • INC-20A declaration of commencement of business
  • ADT-1 first and subsequent auditor appointment
  • Company KYC

Changes to your company

Event-based filings when something about the company changes, with a partner Company Secretary where the law requires one.

  • Change of registered office
  • Appointment or resignation of directors
  • Increase in authorised capital
  • Change of name or objects
  • Amendments and corrections in MCA records

Conversion & closure

Moving to a different structure as the business grows, or closing a company or LLP that is no longer needed.

  • Conversion: private to public, OPC to private, firm or company to LLP
  • Strike-off / closure of company
  • LLP closure

Every ROC form is signed with a director's Class 3 DSC. As an eMudhra partner we issue them directly, usually the same day. DSC details.

Questions

What are the annual ROC filings for a private limited company?

Every company files its financial statements in Form AOC-4 within 30 days of the AGM and its annual return in Form MGT-7 (MGT-7A for small companies and OPCs) within 60 days. Directors also complete DIR-3 KYC each year. For FY 2025-26, MCA General Circular 05/2026 extended AOC-4 to 31 December 2026 and MGT-7 / MGT-7A to 31 January 2027 without additional fees.

What happens if annual filings are missed?

An additional fee of ₹100 per day per form applies, with no upper cap, and repeated defaults can lead to directors being disqualified or the company being struck off. It is usually far cheaper to file late than not at all, so talk to us even if you are behind.

Can you close a company that is no longer doing business?

Yes. We review pending filings and liabilities, bring the company up to date where needed, and file the strike-off application with a partner Company Secretary.
