Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how we work, what it costs and the most common GST and tax questions.

Working with MyFinMantra

Are you a Chartered Accountant firm?

No. MyFinMantra is an independent practice run by an enrolled GST Practitioner. We handle GST, TDS, income tax returns, bookkeeping and MIS directly. Where a CA's audit report or certification is legally required, we coordinate with independent qualified professionals and tell you upfront.

Can you work with businesses outside Gurugram?

Yes. Almost everything is done online — documents over WhatsApp, email or a shared folder, and calls over phone or video. Clients in Gurugram can also meet us in person by appointment.

How do I share documents securely?

Over WhatsApp or email for day-to-day documents, or a shared folder (Google Drive or OneDrive) that only you and we can access. We never ask for your passwords by message; portal access is set up with you on a call.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer or UPI against an invoice. Monthly packages are billed at the start of each month; one-time services are billed 50% upfront and 50% on completion.

Digital Signature Certificates

Can you issue a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)?

Yes. MyFinMantra is an eMudhra partner and issues Class 3 individual, organisation and DGFT DSCs, usually the same day after online verification. A DSC is needed for MCA filings, e-tenders, GeM and DGFT, and for electronically signing income tax and GST returns of companies and LLPs.

What do I need to get a DSC?

Your PAN, Aadhaar (or another ID and address proof), and a mobile number and email for verification. Verification is done online, including a short video taken on your phone. For an organisation DSC, we also need the entity's PAN and an authorisation letter.

Pricing

What does a monthly package cost?

Packages start at ₹4,999* a month for GST returns and bookkeeping up to 100 transactions. The final fee depends on your transaction volume and the services you need; you get a fixed quote before we start.

Do your prices include GST?

No. All fees are exclusive of GST and any government, portal or third-party charges.

What if my case is more complex?

We review it first and quote a fixed fee in writing. You decide before any work starts.

GST

Do I need GST registration?

Generally yes if aggregate turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services (lower in some special-category states), or if you make inter-state supplies, sell through e-commerce platforms, or fall in another compulsory category.

What is QRMP?

The Quarterly Return Monthly Payment scheme lets businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly while paying tax monthly.

I received a GST notice. Can you help?

Yes. Send us a copy of the notice and your returns for the period. We'll explain what it means, what's needed and the fee for preparing the reply, usually within one working day.

Income tax

Which tax regime is better for me?

If you claim few deductions, the new regime is usually better — taxable income up to ₹12 lakh is effectively tax-free. Large deductions like home-loan interest and 80C investments can make the old regime cheaper. Try our calculator for a quick comparison.

What is a 'Tax Year'?

Under the Income-tax Act 2025, in force from 1 April 2026, 'Tax Year' replaces both 'previous year' and 'assessment year'. Tax Year 2026-27 is the period from April 2026 to March 2027.
