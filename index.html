Common questions

Are you a Chartered Accountant firm?

No. MyFinMantra is an independent practice run by an enrolled GST Practitioner. We handle GST, TDS, income tax returns, bookkeeping and MIS directly. Where a CA's audit report or certification is legally required, we coordinate with independent qualified professionals and tell you upfront.

Can you work with businesses outside Gurugram?

Yes. Almost everything is done online — documents over WhatsApp, email or a shared folder, and calls over phone or video. Clients in Gurugram can also meet us in person by appointment.

What does a monthly package cost?

Packages start at ₹4,999* a month for GST returns and bookkeeping up to 100 transactions. The final fee depends on your transaction volume and the services you need; you get a fixed quote before we start.

I received a GST notice. Can you help?

Yes. Send us a copy of the notice and your returns for the period. We'll explain what it means, what's needed and the fee for preparing the reply, usually within one working day.
