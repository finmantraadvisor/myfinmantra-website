Questions

Which tax regime should I choose?

The new regime is the default and usually better if you have few deductions — income up to ₹12 lakh (₹12.75 lakh for salaried people, after the ₹75,000 standard deduction) is effectively tax-free. If you claim large deductions such as home-loan interest, 80C investments and health insurance, the old regime may still be cheaper. Use our calculator or ask us to compare both for you.

I'm a freelancer. Do I need to pay advance tax?

If your tax for the year after TDS is ₹10,000 or more, advance tax applies — in instalments by 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March. Freelancers and professionals opting for presumptive taxation can generally pay the full amount by 15 March.

What happens if I've received an income tax notice?

Don't ignore it — most notices have a response deadline. Share the notice and your return; we'll explain what triggered it and prepare the response.
