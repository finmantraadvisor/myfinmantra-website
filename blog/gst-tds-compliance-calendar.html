Monthly GST, TDS and payroll due dates: a calendar for small businesses

Every recurring due date a small business in India needs to track — GST, TDS, PF, ESI and advance tax — in one place.

By Ankit · 23 October 2026 · 5 min read

Missing a due date usually costs more than the work itself — late fees, interest and, for GST, blocked credit for your customers. These are the recurring dates most small businesses need to track. Dates can be extended by government notification, so always check the portal near the deadline.

Every month

Due byWhatWho
7thDeposit TDS / TCS deducted in the previous month (30 April for March)Anyone deducting TDS
11thGSTR-1 — outward suppliesMonthly GST filers
13thIFF (optional) for the first two months of a quarterQRMP filers
15thPF and ESI contributions for the previous month's salariesEmployers registered with EPFO / ESIC
20thGSTR-3B — summary return and tax paymentMonthly GST filers
25thPMT-06 tax payment for the first two months of a quarterQRMP filers

Every quarter

Due byWhat
13th of the month after the quarterGSTR-1 for QRMP filers
22nd or 24th of the month after the quarterGSTR-3B for QRMP filers — the 24th applies in Haryana, Delhi, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and other northern and eastern states; the 22nd in western and southern states
31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 MayQuarterly TDS statements

Every year

Due byWhat
15 June, 15 September, 15 December, 15 MarchAdvance tax instalments — 15%, 45%, 75% and 100% of the year's estimated tax
31 DecemberGSTR-9 annual return for the previous financial year (where applicable)

Income tax return due dates depend on whether your accounts need an audit and on the return form, and are announced each year — we send reminders to all clients.
